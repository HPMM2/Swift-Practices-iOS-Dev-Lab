## 📁 Apps

### 🔹 01 — Character

A SwiftUI character built entirely with geometric shapes. Uses `ZStack`, `Circle`, and `RoundedRectangle` to compose a face with eyes, nose, mouth and hair using color layers and offsets.

**Concepts practiced:** `ZStack`, `Circle`, `RoundedRectangle`, `.trim()`, `.offset()`, `.frame()`

---

### 🔹 02 — Welcome Page

A swipeable onboarding-style app with three pages displayed in a `TabView` with page style. Includes a welcome screen, a weather card and a bunny showcase card.

**Concepts practiced:** `TabView`, `.tabViewStyle(.page)`, `VStack`, `HStack`, `ZStack`, `Image(systemName:)`, `.symbolRenderingMode()`, `DisclosureGroup`

---

### 🔹 03 — App (BioHM)

A personal presentation app with a bottom tab bar navigation. Features a home screen with profile photo and name, a scrollable biography section and a favorites page with hobbies, food and colors.

**Concepts practiced:** `TabView` with `.tabItem`, `ScrollView`, `DisclosureGroup`, `Divider`, `Image` with `.clipShape(Circle())`, `.overlay()`, custom backgrounds

---

### 🔹 04 — Glosario

An animated home screen with letter-by-letter wave animation on the title, a circular profile image surrounded by 360 pulsing lines and circular text. Navigates to a glossary view using `NavigationStack`.

**Concepts practiced:** `NavigationStack`, `NavigationPath`, `.navigationDestination`, `@State`, `.animation()`, `.rotationEffect()`, `ForEach`, hover effects, `LinearGradient`
